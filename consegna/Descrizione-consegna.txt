Flappy Bird 3D - optional ML-Agents assignment

The game builds on Flappy Bird 3D from Lab 3 and uses the Sparrow and Pipe assets
provided in the course. The agent was trained with PPO and decides when to flap
to pass through the pipes. Golden orbs grant extra lives, while each collision
costs one life.

The network uses the bird's position and velocity, together with information
about the upcoming pipes and prizes.

In the final evaluation, the agent cleared 40 pipe pairs in all 30 runs
without a collision. It collected an average of about 13 prizes per run.
